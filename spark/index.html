How it works

Small gestures, every day.

Love lives in the little things. Spark makes them easy to remember and fun to do.

  1. Every day: three small gesturesFrom three different kinds of care. Most take under 15 minutes and cost nothing.
  2. Every week: three date ideasFrom cozy to adventurous. Pick one and enjoy it together before Sunday.
  3. Keep your memoriesSave the dates you’ve had in a memory book, with a photo and a note if you like.
What makes it different

Deeds, not questionnaires.

Variety instead of labels

Kind words, quality time, helpful acts, small gifts and closeness: a balanced mix every day, no personality test.

Works on your own

Your partner doesn’t need the app. One of you is enough to make a difference.

Private, no account

Your gestures and memories stay on your phone.

A gentle daily streak

A reason to check in every day, without levels, points or scores.

Five kinds of dates

Active, relaxing, emotional, creative and culinary, so every week feels different.

Hand-picked ideas

Every gesture and every date is chosen and checked by hand before it goes into the app.

Privacy

What happens between you two stays between you two.

Spark is being built to work without an account and to keep everything on your device. A full privacy policy will be published here before launch.
