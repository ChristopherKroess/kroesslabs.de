Every day, a little adventure.

Your evening deserves better than another hour of scrolling. Quevia gives you one small, real-life adventure a day.

How it works Privacy policy

Quevia is in a closed test on Google Play right now. The public release on Google Play and the App Store follows soon.

How it works

Pick one. Go do it. Level up.

Every morning Quevia offers three Daily Quests. Choose one, do it, and check it off in the evening. That’s it.

  1. Every morning: three Daily QuestsPick one, and it’s yours for the day.
  2. Every week: three Weekly QuestsBigger ones, with the whole week to do them.
  3. Done? Check it offEarn XP, level up and keep your daily streak going. Tapped the wrong one? Undo it right away.
A look inside

Simple on the surface. Thoughtful underneath.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don’t.

133 quests in five categories

Social, Body, Creativity, Mind and Adventure. Quests you’ve completed don’t come back, so there’s always something new.

Five minutes or a little courage

Smile at three strangers today, or set up a full sit-down dinner somewhere outdoors, like a forest or a rooftop.

Reminders that know when to stay quiet

One nudge in the morning to pick, one in the evening to confirm. Skipped when there’s nothing left to do.

Private by design

No account. No ads. No tracking. Quevia doesn’t even ask for internet access: everything stays on your phone.

Your adventure archive

A searchable archive of everything you’ve done, with your XP, level and streak.

15 languages, light and dark

Switch the language right in the app, and pick light or dark mode.

Privacy

Your adventures stay on your phone.

Quevia stores your quests and progress only on your device. The Android app doesn’t even request internet access, and the iOS app makes no network connections.
