Your health data, in your own hands.

BerryVault continuously exports your Apple Health data to a destination you choose: your own server, like a Raspberry Pi at home, or your OneDrive. No middleman, no lock-in.

How it works

BerryVault is in development for iPhone and not yet available on the App Store.

How it works

Set it up once. Then forget about it.

BerryVault runs quietly in the background and keeps your own copy of your health data up to date.

  1. Allow accessChoose which Health data BerryVault may read. It never writes to Apple Health.
  2. Pick a destinationYour own server on your home network, or a private app folder in your OneDrive.
  3. Let it syncBerryVault sends new data when you open the app, unlock your iPhone or Health reports new samples, and retries on its own if a transfer fails.
Features

Complete, careful and yours.

Everything Apple Health offers

Sleep and sleep stages, heart rate and HRV, workouts and routes, ECGs, nutrition, mood and much more: over 190 data types.

Only what’s new

Each sync sends just what was added, changed or deleted since last time. Deletions arrive exactly, without duplicates.

Built for reliability

Data is only removed from the queue once every destination has confirmed it. Unfinished uploads complete in the background.

Your server or OneDrive

Send your data to your own server at home, or to a folder in OneDrive that only BerryVault can reach.

Clear when something’s wrong

If a login expires or your server can’t be reached, BerryVault tells you exactly what to do.

Read-only and private

BerryVault only reads your data and sends it nowhere but to the destinations you set up.

Privacy

No cloud in between.

Your health data goes straight from your iPhone to your own destination. There is no BerryVault server and no account. A full privacy policy will be published here before launch.
